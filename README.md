# 23 November 2026 — The World in One Image

A newspaper front page summing up the world news of Monday, 23 November 2026.

## What the front page covers

- **Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO** — shares jump about 33% and the S&P 500 rises 4.8% in a sharp Wall Street rebound after last week's AI sell-off.
- **Iran vows to fight the U.S. blockade** — Tehran says it will use all available means to counter the naval blockade as tensions rise in the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Democrats win their biggest House majority since 1992** — at least 258 seats in the U.S. House of Representatives.
- **Ukraine sinks a Russian warship off Libya** — naval drones sink the corvette *Merkury* in Benghazi port, Kyiv says.
- **Russian strike kills 11 in a Ukrainian town** — a missile hits an apartment block in Dymer, north of Kyiv.
- **Climate talks end without a deal** — COP31 in Turkey closes as the longest UN climate summit in history, with no agreement on fossil fuels.
- **Trump calls Putin "my friend" after summit** — the U.S. president meets Putin and Xi Jinping in Shenzhen.
